Drone Logistics for Minna, Abuja and Nigeria

We build 2 to 3 kg delivery drones locally, then use them to bring food, groceries and provisions to customers in about 15 minutes.

The problem

When someone orders food or provisions, the wait is often far longer than promised. Traffic, bad roads, few available riders and long routes between vendor and customer all add delay.

The food arrives late and often cold. Customers lose patience, vendors lose repeat orders, and small deliveries within the same neighbourhood take as long as long-distance ones.

The solution

An in-house built drone flies straight from vendor to customer, over the traffic. After an order is placed, delivery of food or any item up to 2 to 3 kg takes about 15 minutes.

That covers meals, groceries, provisions, drinks, pharmacy items and small parcels within the service area.

Business model

₦1.5MCost to build one 2 to 3 kg drone, with 4 years of maintenance included
₦1,000Charge per delivery within the service area
₦20,000Daily revenue at 20 deliveries per drone
₦600,000Monthly revenue per drone (₦20,000 × 30 days)

We fabricate each drone ourselves. The ₦1.5M cost covers the build and also battery replacements and spare parts for up to 4 years, so there are no surprise repair bills. Revenue comes from a flat ₦1,000 fee on every delivery made for food vendors, grocery shops and provision stores.
